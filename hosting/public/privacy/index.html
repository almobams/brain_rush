BRAIN RUSH

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Brain Rush is a mobile brain game. This page explains what the app stores on your device, what its optional online ranking sends, and how ads, purchases, sharing, and reminders work. Questions about this policy can be sent to almobams1@gmail.com.

Information kept on your device

The app saves game progress, scores, Daily Challenge attempts, preferences, a chosen language, and an optional player name on your device. The name is for personalizing your own app experience. It is not sent to the ranking service or linked to your ranking identifier. You can edit or remove it in Settings. Removing the app or clearing its data generally removes local progress and preferences.

Anonymous Daily ranking

If you play Daily Challenge, the app uses a randomly generated installation identifier to submit the challenge date, best score, and attempt number to a Supabase-hosted database. The ranking service returns your rank and score information. No account or public username is used. The installation identifier is not a guarantee of anonymity against all parties: it is a persistent pseudonymous ID until app data is cleared. Network providers may also process connection information such as an IP address. Reinstalling or clearing app data may generate a new ID and disconnect the old ranking record from your device.

Ads and privacy choices

Brain Rush uses Google Mobile Ads to show ads where eligible. Google and its partners may process device identifiers, IP addresses, and other data for ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention according to their own policies and your consent choices. Where required, the app presents Google’s User Messaging Platform consent flow; Privacy choices may also be available in Settings. Buying Remove Ads suppresses interstitial ads in the app. See how Google uses information from partner apps.

Purchases

The optional Remove Ads purchase is handled by Apple App Store or Google Play. The app receives purchase status to determine entitlement and restore purchases. Brain Rush does not ask you to enter payment-card details.

Sharing and reminders

A result image is generated on your device. It may include your local player name if you set one. The image is sent to another app only after you tap Share and choose a destination in the system share sheet. Daily reminders, if you opt in, are scheduled locally on your device. Their text may include your local name. They do not use push tokens or a notification server, and you can switch them off in Brain Rush Settings or device settings.

This website

The Brain Rush website does not use analytics, advertising scripts, forms, or cookies set by our page code. Firebase Hosting serves the pages and may process ordinary request information such as IP address and browser details as part of delivering the site. Visit Firebase privacy information for more about Google’s handling of that data.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions or requests concerning Brain Rush, email almobams1@gmail.com. We may update this policy when the app or its providers change; the date above will show the latest revision.
